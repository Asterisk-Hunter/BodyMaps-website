BodyMaps

The intelligence layer for medical imaging AI

Browse the library in 2D and 3D, upload your own CT for AI segmentation, and annotate or refine the results.

What you can do here

  • CT library: search body CT scans from public research datasets, including PanTS and CancerVerse, filter them, and open any scan in a 2D and 3D viewer with its organ and tumor labels.
  • Statistics: interactive charts of the library (contrast phase, sex, age at scan, scanner make and model, tumor status, country) that follow your search filters.
  • Upload a CT (NIfTI or a DICOM series) and run AI segmentation, then review, annotate and refine the result in the browser.

Segmentation models

  • ePAI: full abdominal organ segmentation, with detailed pancreas and tumor analysis. 25 structures in one pass, including the pancreas gland, duct and 3 tumor subtypes (PDAC, cyst, PNET).
  • Atlas-Net: the same organ and tumor coverage as ePAI, with segmentations matched to a known anatomical atlas for anatomically consistent results.
  • LesionSegmenter: fast pancreatic lesion detection, covering lesions in 4 organs in one pass.

About

BodyMaps is developed by a research team at Johns Hopkins University led by Zongwei Zhou, PhD, with Alan L. Yuille, PhD as scientific advisor.
